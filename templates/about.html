For shop owners

Let your neighbourhood discover your shop.

Create a personal account, then register your business using the same account. Add your shop logo, banner, products and offers, and manage your shop from your dashboard.

Your first 10 products are free, with up to 2 images per product. Need more product slots? Contact the LOCALFROM admin to request a higher allowance.

You can also apply to advertise your business. Ad applications and submitted offers go through admin review.
