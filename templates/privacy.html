Privacy Policy

What information LOCALFROM uses, who can see it and how to contact us about your data.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy covers information handled through LOCALFROM, our local platform starting in Vandavasi, Tamil Nadu. It applies to visitors, members, shop owners, news contributors and Local Ride participants.

The LOCALFROM team handles privacy enquiries through localfrom8@gmail.com. This policy describes the service; it does not mean that a person's account or content has been independently verified.

2. Information you provide

  • Account details: your name, phone number, password and pincode/city. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable text. Other profile details, such as a photo, email or address, are used if you provide them.
  • Shop information: business name, category, address, contact details, logo, banner, product photos, prices, descriptions, discounts, offers, videos and advertising applications.
  • Messages: conversations, attachments, participants, timestamps and read status needed to provide messaging.
  • Local News: submitted text, photos, account/contributor details and editorial decisions. Signed-in news submission uses your account phone number. Separate contributor verification forms, where offered, also request a name, address, pincode, identity document and profile photo. A masked Aadhaar is accepted in that form.
  • Local Ride: journey start and destination, date/time, vehicle type, seats, notes, join requests and their status.
  • Interactions and support: shop follows, reviews or other feedback you submit, product-access requests and information you choose to send to support.

Only submit information or images about others when you are entitled to do so. Ordinary account registration does not require an identity-document upload.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create accounts, authenticate access, show content for your selected area, run listings, deliver messages and notifications, review news and advertising, coordinate Local Ride requests, provide support and protect the service against misuse.

Pincode and city help select nearby content. These features do not require continuous GPS tracking. A demo signup code, where displayed, does not prove phone ownership and must not be treated as verified identity.

We also use operational records and usage statistics to understand which features work, diagnose errors and respond to security or legal issues. We do not sell personal information.

4. Who can see your information

Listings and published activity

Shop profiles, business contact details, products, approved offers/ads, published news, reviews and journey posts are visible to people who can access those features. An account requirement does not make a listing confidential. People may copy or share content they can view.

Messages and submissions awaiting review

Private conversations and their attachments are intended for the participants. They are stored by the service and are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised service operators may need access for support, security investigations or legal requirements; private does not mean inaccessible to the operator.

Unpublished news submissions are available to the contributor and authorised editorial/admin users. Contributor identity documents and review photos are restricted to authorised review access and are not intended for publication with a story. Keep private details out of news text or images intended for publication.

Service administration

Authorised admins can access account and shop information needed for moderation, support and management. Superadmin can view member-related usage statistics. Ride request details are shown according to journey and request permissions.

5. Cookies, browser storage and analytics

Session and security cookies support sign-in and protect forms. Browser storage remembers settings such as language and display preferences. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in or other features from working.

First-party analytics use a browser identifier to count visits, page views, button/link clicks, form-submit actions and visible browsing time. This identifier cookie can last up to one year. Activity can be linked to your member account when signed in, and admins can view the statistics. Separate browsers or devices may be counted separately.

These first-party event records do not store IP addresses, passwords, OTPs, form values, search text or private message contents. They honour browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. This describes first-party analytics, not every server log or third-party tool.

Where configured, Google Analytics (GA4) may also load on pages with its tracking tag, including account pages. It processes browser/device and interaction information under Google's Privacy Policy. First-party analytics settings are not a separate consent control for GA4. You can manage cookies and tracking through your browser settings.

6. Technical records and service providers

Web servers and security tools may record IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information, timestamps, response codes and error details for operation and security. Do not put secrets in URLs or public posts.

Hosting, storage, backup and other configured providers process information needed to operate the site. If external error monitoring is enabled, it may receive diagnostic information. SMS providers receive a phone number and delivery information when real SMS delivery is configured; a visible demo code is not an SMS.

Provider processing or storage may take place outside your local area or India, depending on the service used. We may disclose relevant information when required by law or necessary to respond to fraud, abuse or a security incident. External links, including Instagram and WhatsApp, have their own privacy practices.

7. Storage, security and retention

We use measures such as password hashing and access restrictions to protect information. No online service can guarantee complete security. Keep your password private and contact us if you suspect unauthorised access.

We retain information while needed to provide accounts and services, manage moderation, address disputes or security issues, and meet applicable legal requirements. Retention depends on the record and purpose; this policy does not promise immediate deletion or a fixed deletion period for every category.

Removing a listing from view does not necessarily erase its records or files. Backup copies may remain until replaced or removed through the backup lifecycle. Recipients may retain copies of information you shared. Contact us to discuss deletion of a particular record.

8. Your choices and data requests

You can update your local area through My account and use available profile/shop tools to correct information. For access, correction, account closure, deletion, withdrawal of consent where relevant, or a privacy complaint, email localfrom8@gmail.com with the subject LOCALFROM privacy request.

Tell us what you want changed and the account or record concerned. We may request proportionate information to confirm that the request is yours. Do not send passwords, OTPs or unrequested identity documents. We will assess requests and respond in accordance with applicable law, explaining any legal, security or other valid reason that limits what we can do.

Choosing not to provide information needed for a feature, or requesting its removal, may mean that feature or account can no longer be provided. Questions and complaints can be raised through the same contact channel.

9. Children and policy updates

Account registration is intended for people aged 18 or over. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an account, contact us so we can review the situation.

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated through the service where appropriate.
